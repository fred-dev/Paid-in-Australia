# Source Notes

## Source hierarchy

Use sources in this order where possible:

1. legislation
2. official regulator guidance
3. official procurement guidance
4. parliamentary inquiries and audit reports
5. Productivity Commission and similar public bodies
6. peer reviewed research
7. transparent sector reports
8. media reporting
9. opinion and commentary

## Citation rules

Each factual claim should be linked to a source.

Each modelling assumption should identify whether it is:

- directly evidenced
- inferred
- estimated
- speculative
- pending data

## Legal sources to add

- Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993
