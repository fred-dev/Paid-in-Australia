# Contributing

Contributions are welcome if they strengthen the policy, evidence, modelling, drafting, or implementation pathway.

## Contribution standards

A useful contribution should do at least one of the following:

- clarify an existing proposal
- add reliable evidence
- correct a legal or factual error
- identify a risk or unintended consequence
- improve the neutrality of the wording
- add a sector specific implementation pathway
- improve modelling assumptions
- propose a more workable threshold or test
- convert a broad proposal into precise policy language

## Evidence standards

When making factual claims, use reliable sources. Preferred sources include:

- legislation
- official government guidance
- regulator guidance
- parliamentary reports
- Productivity Commission material
- audit reports
- peer reviewed research
- reputable sector reports with transparent methods

Opinion pieces may be useful for understanding political arguments, but should not be treated as primary evidence.

## Tone

Documents should be clear, firm, and non-party-political. The project is designed to be legible to people across the political spectrum.

Avoid framing that relies on partisan identity. The preferred framing is public value, Australian circulation of public money, fair competition, secure work, lower leakage, better stewardship, and practical institutional design.

## Pull request checklist

Before submitting a pull request, check that it:

- does not contradict the fixed commitments in `GOVERNANCE.md`
- separates evidence from opinion
- cites sources for factual claims
- identifies implementation risks where relevant
- uses Australian English
- avoids overclaiming
- keeps the proposal legally serious

## Legal caution

This repository develops policy proposals. It does not provide legal advice. Legislative drafting and procurement design will require review by qualified lawyers and relevant experts.
