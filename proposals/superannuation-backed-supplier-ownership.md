# Proposal: Superannuation Backed Supplier Ownership

## Status

MVP proposal note requiring legal and financial review.

## Summary

Develop mechanisms for Australian superannuation funds to invest in government supplier markets where this can produce risk adjusted returns for members and keep more public value circulating in Australia.

## Rationale

Government funded markets can produce predictable revenue. Some suppliers may be suitable for long term investment if they meet governance, transparency, and risk standards.

## Legal caution

Superannuation funds cannot be used as a public policy instrument at the expense of member interests. Any model must comply with trustee duties, investment governance, diversification, liquidity needs, and the sole purpose test.

## Initial steps

1. Start with disclosure of superannuation ownership in government suppliers.
2. Identify sectors with stable long term revenue and investable characteristics.
3. Explore pooled investment vehicles to avoid concentration of control.
4. Consider government co-investment or credit support where it improves risk adjusted returns.
5. Model member outcomes and public value retention.

## Open questions

- Which supplier sectors are genuinely investable for superannuation funds?
- How can investment be voluntary, prudent, and member focused?
- Could procurement scoring recognise Australian-benefit ownership without breaching procurement commitments?
- What governance rules prevent political allocation of retirement savings?
