# Case Studies

This document will gather sector and international examples relevant to the policy.

## Candidate Australian sectors

### Early childhood education and care

Questions:

- How do public, not-for-profit, and for-profit providers compare on quality ratings?
- How does provider type affect wages, workforce stability, and fees?
- What role does government subsidy play in provider revenue?

### NDIS and disability services

Questions:

- Which services are most exposed to profit leakage or low quality provision?
- Which provider types produce better participant outcomes?
- How could Public-Benefit Supplier status interact with NDIS registration?

### Consulting and professional services, later-stage

Questions:

- Which consulting functions could be provided by Public-Benefit Suppliers?
- Could skilled workers leave large firms and form lean not-for-distribution consultancies?
- How much public spending currently flows to high overhead corporate structures?

### Maintenance and small infrastructure

Questions:

- Which contracts are suitable for local Public-Benefit Suppliers?
- Could worker led entities compete with large contractors?
- How can insurance and compliance barriers be reduced?
