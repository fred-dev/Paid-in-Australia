# Research Evidence

This document gathers evidence for the policy. It should distinguish evidence from assumptions and claims.

## Current evidence base

### Procurement can consider broader value

The Commonwealth Procurement Rules identify value for money as a core requirement and include broader considerations such as economic benefit, competition, ethical procurement, accountability, transparency, and risk.

Source notes:

- Commonwealth Procurement Rules: https://www.finance.gov.au/government/procurement/commonwealth-procurement-rules
- Value for money guidance: https://www.finance.gov.au/government/procurement/commonwealth-procurement-rules/value-money
- Economic benefits guidance: https://www.finance.gov.au/government/procurement/consideration-economic-benefits-procurement

### Company limited by guarantee is a relevant reference structure

ASIC describes companies limited by guarantee as a common structure for not-for-profit and charitable organisations. Companies limited by guarantee registered on or after 28 June 2010 cannot pay dividends to members.

Source notes:

- ASIC, registering and structuring not-for-profit organisations: https://www.asic.gov.au/for-business-and-companies/nfps-and-charities/registering-and-structuring-not-for-profit-organisations/
- ASIC, obligations of companies limited by guarantee: https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/financial-reporting-and-audit/preparers-of-financial-reports/companies-limited-by-guarantee/obligations-of-companies-limited-by-guarantee/

### Charity governance is useful by analogy but not the proposed status

ACNC governance standards apply to registered charities. Public-Benefit Supplier status should not require charitable purpose, but ACNC governance standards are useful as examples of accountability, responsible governance, and lawful operation.

Source notes:

- ACNC governance standards: https://www.acnc.gov.au/tools/topic-guides/governance-standards

### Superannuation investment must serve members

APRA guidance on the sole purpose test says the retirement income objective is paramount. This creates a major constraint on any proposal involving mandatory or incentivised superannuation investment.

Source notes:

- APRA, sole purpose test letter: https://handbook.apra.gov.au/letter/1/letter-rse-licensees-sole-purpose-test
- Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993: https://www.legislation.gov.au/Series/C2004A04633

### Secure employment requirements should be designed with Fair Work law

Fair Work guidance recognises pathways from casual to permanent employment. Public-Benefit Supplier rules should be designed consistently with the Fair Work Act, National Employment Standards, awards, and enterprise agreements.

Source notes:

- Fair Work, casual employees: https://www.fairwork.gov.au/starting-employment/types-of-employees/casual-employees
- Fair Work, becoming a permanent employee: https://www.fairwork.gov.au/starting-employment/types-of-employees/casual-employees/becoming-a-permanent-employee

### NDIS is a candidate sector because it already has provider regulation

The NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission registers and regulates NDIS providers and works to improve the quality and safety of supports and services. This makes disability services a potential sector for testing supplier status and public-benefit obligations.

Source notes:

- NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission: https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/


### Government procurement spending requires careful measurement

Commonwealth AusTender contract values are a useful starting point, but they do not equal annual cash expenditure. Contract values may represent maximum whole-of-life values and may span multiple years. The project should therefore build separate measures for reported contract value, estimated annualised value, and actual expenditure where data is available.

Initial source notes:

- Department of Finance, Statistics on Australian Government Procurement Contracts: https://www.finance.gov.au/government/procurement/statistics-australian-government-procurement-contracts-
- ANAO, Australian Government Procurement Contract Reporting 2022 update: https://www.anao.gov.au/work/information/australian-government-procurement-contract-reporting-2022-update
- AusTender reports: https://www.tenders.gov.au/reports/list

### Contractor profit and tax analysis should be evidence-led

The project should identify major government contractors using procurement data, then compare those suppliers with annual reports, ASIC records, ATO corporate tax transparency data, and ownership data. Low or zero tax paid should be treated as a research flag, not as proof of wrongdoing.

Initial source notes:

- ATO Corporate Tax Transparency Report 2023-24: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/corporate-tax-measures-and-assurance/large-business/in-detail/tax-transparency/corporate-tax-transparency-report-2023-24
- ATO corporate tax transparency reports: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/corporate-tax-measures-and-assurance/large-business/corporate-tax-transparency/our-corporate-tax-transparency-reports
- data.gov.au corporate tax transparency dataset: https://data.gov.au/data/dataset/corporate-transparency

## Evidence still needed

The repository needs more evidence on:

- government spending at Commonwealth, state, territory, and local levels
- top government contractors by value, contract count, category, agency, jurisdiction, and panel use
- profit margins by sector
- executive remuneration in major government suppliers
- offshore ownership and dividend leakage
- subcontracting chains
- comparative quality between for-profit, not-for-profit, public, mutual, and cooperative providers
- workforce casualisation in government funded markets
- cost overheads in large corporate providers
- successful mutual, cooperative, and not-for-distribution supplier models
- international procurement preference models
- legal limits on domestic ownership preference
- superannuation investment in infrastructure and operating companies
- likely fiscal effects

## Evidence rules

Each claim should be tagged as one of:

- established source
- plausible inference
- modelling assumption
- policy judgement
- unresolved question
