# Legal Map

This document identifies legal and regulatory areas that must be reviewed. It is not legal advice.

## Procurement law

Relevant sources include:

- Commonwealth Procurement Rules
- Government Procurement Judicial Review Act 2018
- state and territory procurement frameworks
- local government procurement rules
- free trade agreement procurement chapters
- WTO Agreement on Government Procurement commitments

Key questions:

- Can procurement scoring prefer Public-Benefit Suppliers?
- Can some categories be reserved for Public-Benefit Suppliers?
- What rules apply above procurement thresholds?
- How can broader economic benefit be assessed lawfully?
- What non-discrimination obligations apply?

## Corporations and entity law

Relevant sources include:

- Corporations Act 2001
- company limited by guarantee rules
- cooperatives and mutuals legislation
- incorporated associations legislation
- director duties
- reporting obligations

Key questions:

- What entity forms can support no-distribution obligations?
- Is a new Commonwealth statutory status required?
- Can the status attach to multiple entity forms?
- What constitutional limits are needed?

## Charity and not-for-profit law

The proposal is not charity reform. ACNC rules are still useful as a governance reference point, but Public-Benefit Supplier status should not require charitable purpose.

Key questions:

- Which ACNC governance concepts are useful by analogy?
- Which charity concepts should be avoided?
- Should a separate regulator be created, or should an existing regulator administer the status?

## Labour law

Relevant sources include:

- Fair Work Act 2009
- National Employment Standards
- modern awards
- enterprise agreements
- casual employment rules
- wage theft and underpayment provisions

Key questions:

- How can secure employment requirements be attached to supplier status?
- What limited exceptions to permanent employment are needed?
- How should pay ratio rules be calculated?

## Superannuation law

Relevant sources include:

- Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993
- APRA prudential standards
- trustee duties
- sole purpose test
- retirement income covenant

Key questions:

- Can superannuation funds be incentivised to invest in Australian-benefit suppliers without breaching member duties?
- Can pooled vehicles reduce risk and concentration?
- Can government co-investment improve risk adjusted returns?
- What forms of ownership preference would be unlawful or impractical?

## Competition and consumer law

Relevant sources include:

- Competition and Consumer Act 2010
- cartel provisions
- misuse of market power provisions
- merger control
- consumer protection law

Key questions:

- Could supplier preference reduce competition?
- Could superannuation ownership concentration create competition concerns?
- How should multiple fund ownership be structured to avoid control problems?

## Constitutional and federalism questions

Key questions:

- Which reforms can be made federally through procurement policy?
- Which require legislation?
- Which require state and territory cooperation?
- Which can begin through local government procurement?

## Criminal law

The MVP does not propose a bespoke imprisonment regime for regulatory breach.

Serious misconduct should be referred to existing criminal law pathways only where the conduct may already constitute an offence, such as fraud, bribery, corruption, false accounting, wage theft, or deliberate evasion.
