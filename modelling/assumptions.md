# Modelling Assumptions

Assumptions should be explicit, testable, and easy to revise.

## Placeholder assumptions

- Public-Benefit Suppliers may reduce profit extraction but may increase compliance costs.
- Worker led suppliers may reduce executive overhead but may require startup support.
- Secure employment may increase labour costs but reduce turnover and improve quality.
- Subcontracting caps may reduce leakage but may reduce flexibility in some sectors.
- Australian-benefit ownership may retain more value domestically but must be assessed against investment risk and procurement constraints.

## Required sensitivity tests

Each model should test low, medium, and high cases for:

- profit leakage
- overhead reduction
- wage increase
- compliance cost
- transition cost
- service quality effect
- provider entry rate
- provider failure risk
