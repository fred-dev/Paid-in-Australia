# Scenarios

## Scenario 1: Baseline

Current provider mix, ownership, wages, quality, and profit flows.

## Scenario 2: Transparency only

Large suppliers disclose ownership, profit distribution, offshore payments, subcontracting, executive remuneration, and labour standards.

## Scenario 3: Public-Benefit Supplier preference

Public-Benefit Suppliers receive additional procurement scoring weight in selected sectors.

## Scenario 4: Public-Benefit Supplier mandate

Selected sectors require a defined percentage of government funded work to be delivered by Public-Benefit Suppliers.

## Scenario 5: Australian-benefit ownership preference

For-profit suppliers receive scoring benefits for qualifying Australian-benefit ownership structures.

## Scenario 6: Combined pathway

Public-Benefit Supplier preference applies to suitable smaller and service intensive sectors, while Australian-benefit ownership preference applies to larger capital intensive sectors.
