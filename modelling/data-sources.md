# Data Sources

Potential data sources include:

- AusTender
- state and territory procurement portals
- local government procurement data
- ASIC company data
- APRA superannuation statistics
- ABS labour and industry statistics
- NDIS provider and expenditure data
- early childhood education and care quality data
- annual reports of major suppliers
- parliamentary inquiries
- audit reports
- Fair Work Ombudsman data
- Productivity Commission reports

Each data source should be assessed for coverage, reliability, timeliness, cost, and licence conditions.


## Procurement and contractor evaluation data

Priority data sources for the next stage:

- AusTender contract notices and annual reports
- Department of Finance procurement statistics
- ANAO procurement reporting analysis
- state and territory procurement portals
- local council annual reports and tender disclosures
- ATO corporate tax transparency data
- data.gov.au corporate transparency dataset
- ASIC and ABR records
- company annual reports and ASX filings
- modern slavery statements for supplier-chain information

The model should distinguish reported contract value, annualised contract value, and actual expenditure.
